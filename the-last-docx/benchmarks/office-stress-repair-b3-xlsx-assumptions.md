# Office DSH Repair Batch 3 — XLSX assumptions log pagination

Date: 2026-09-27  
Status: implemented through DSH Profile and visually reviewed using LibreOffice + Poppler. Microsoft Excel was unavailable; this is not a claim of native Excel rendering equivalence.

## Attention lock

This batch is limited to the `Model inputs and assumptions` worksheet print settings. The `Welcome` worksheet, cell contents, formulas, styles, defined names, links, comments, drawings, and every unrelated OOXML package part are outside the repair scope. Do not add/remove data, invent a print area, hide columns, or force a long sheet onto one page vertically.

## Root cause and route

The frozen preview rendered the two-sheet assumptions log as five pages: two `Welcome` pages and three horizontal fragments of the wide model-inputs form. DSH cell reading was not the issue. The workbook contains `customXml`, drawings, external links, and threaded comments, so whole-workbook rewriting would risk unsupported feature loss.

The repair used the DSH `xlsx-office.execute` `setPrintLayout` action with a package-scoped OOXML edit on only `xl/worksheets/sheet2.xml`: landscape, fit to at most two pages across, and unlimited pages tall (`fitToWidth=2`, `fitToHeight=0`). A one-page-wide trial was rejected because it compressed the wide form materially. Keeping a two-page horizontal continuation is the readability-preserving compromise; its second page is still dense and repeats the colored banner strip without the full title.

## Result

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| LibreOffice print-preview pages | 5 | 4 |
| `Welcome` pages | 2 | 2, unchanged |
| `Model inputs and assumptions` horizontal fragments | 3 | 2 |
| Candidate workbook data changes | — | none reported by DSH verifier |

All four candidate pages were inspected in a contact sheet; both target-sheet pages were also inspected at full 120-DPI resolution. The left and right portions of the table, headers, example row, colored ratings and evidence/date fields remain present. Page 4 remains a horizontal continuation with a blank repeated banner/title strip, and the multi-column text is dense. This is a bounded reduction in pagination fragmentation, not a redesigned form or a single-page print layout.

## Limits

This action stores print setup only; it does not establish that all Excel versions or printers will paginate identically. The workbook still prints its model-input form across two horizontal pages, and page 4 has an incomplete repeated banner. The `Welcome` sheet retains its original two-page layout. No Excel desktop print-preview comparison was available. A further layout redesign would need a separate explicit scope and should not be achieved by deleting or hiding workbook content.
